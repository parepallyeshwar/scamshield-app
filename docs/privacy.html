ScamShield AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

ScamShield AI ("the app") is a free tool that helps you check whether a message is a scam. It is built and maintained by Eshwar Parepally. This policy explains what happens to your data.

1. What you send for a scan

When you tap Scan, the text you pasted (and the screenshot, if you attached one) is sent over HTTPS to our scanning service, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to produce the analysis. The result is sent back to your phone.

  • We do not store your messages or screenshots on our servers, and we do not log their content.
  • Google processes the content under the Gemini API Terms. Depending on the service tier used, Google may retain or use submitted content as described in those terms.
  • Do not paste passwords, OTPs, card numbers or other secrets you don't need checked. Redact them if you can.

If the scanning service is unavailable, the app uses an on-device rule engine and nothing leaves your phone.

2. Device identifier

The app creates a random identifier on first launch. It is used only to rate-limit requests and prevent abuse. It is not linked to your name, phone number, or any account, and it is not used for advertising.

3. Scan history

Your scan history is stored only on your device. You can delete it any time from the History tab, or by uninstalling the app.

4. What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads, no advertising IDs, no third-party analytics or tracking
  • No access to your SMS inbox, contacts, location, camera or microphone. Screenshots are read only when you pick one.
  • We don't sell or share data with anyone except as described in section 1

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

6. Disclaimer

ScamShield gives guidance, not guarantees. If in doubt, contact your bank or service provider on their official number. In India, report cyber fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
